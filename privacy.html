Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At LuluTap (accessible from our website), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by LuluTap and how we use it.

Quick Summary: LuluTap is a free online HTML5 gaming platform. You can play games instantly without creating an account or providing personal information.

1. Log Files

LuluTap follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamp
  • Referring/exit pages and possibly the number of clicks

These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, LuluTap uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, game progress/local savings, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on LuluTap, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.

These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that LuluTap has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

LuluTap does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

LuluTap's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to reach out to us through our Contact Us page.
